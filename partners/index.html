Who we work with

Every paid spot is labeled “Sponsored” or “Partner”. Our fair-price check is the same for everyone.

Car dealers

Used and new, sales and service.

  • Shoppers who already know their monthly budget
  • Honest prices get a “Looks fair” tag
  • Service: recall and warning-light visits
  • First month free, then a small fee per ready buyer (planned)

Repair & tire shops

When a light comes on, drivers find you.

  • Your shop at the top of “Find a shop near me”, labeled
  • Your offer, in your words
  • Never inside safety steps
  • First month free, then a small fee per customer (planned)

Credit unions & lenders

Fair car loans. Credit unions first.

  • Shoppers who already know their monthly payment
  • Your rates shown honestly, with a “Partner” label
  • We never take loan applications ourselves
  • Referral per funded loan (planned)

Insurance

The full cost before they buy.

  • Quotes where people check what a car costs to own
  • Clear “Partner” label
  • Only when the driver asks
  • Referral per quote (planned)

Our promise

To drivers

Free, always. Safety help is never an ad. We never sell their information, and we share it only when they choose to contact you.

To partners

No contract to start, no monthly fee in the pilot, a short weekly report, and an honest look at what worked.

Who we are

A small, local team from Atlanta. We built Carvessa so buying and keeping a car feels simple and fair, for drivers and for the businesses that serve them.
