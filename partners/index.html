Questions businesses ask

Straight answers. Ask us anything else on the call.

Is there a contract?

No. The pilot has no contract and no monthly fee. Stop any time with one email.

Do drivers pay to use Carvessa?

No. Carvessa is free for drivers, and safety help is never an ad.

Can I pay to get a better price tag or rating?

No. Our fair-price check and “Looks fair” tags work the same for everyone. Paying never changes them.

How do you mark paid spots?

Every paid spot says “Sponsored” or “Partner”. The closest shops and other options always show too.

What if nobody comes from Carvessa?

Then you don’t pay. You only pay when we send you a ready buyer, a customer or a funded loan, at a price agreed with you first.

What do you share about drivers?

Only what the driver chooses to send when they contact you. We never sell their information.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí. The app works in English and Spanish, and we can call you in Spanish.

How do I get started?

Send the form below. We call you within 2 business days, set up your offer together, and your first month is free.
