Leaking under the car: likely causes and what to do

The most common reasons, how urgent each one is, and when to stop driving.

SAFETY FIRST

If you smell gas or see gas dripping, don’t drive and don’t smoke. Stay away from the car and call for help.

Likely causes

Clear water from the A/C (normal)Most likely
Can wait
Brown or black: oilMaybe
Fix soon
Green, pink or orange: coolantMaybe
Fix soon

What to do

  1. Notice when it happens (speed, braking, turning, cold or warm engine). It helps the mechanic.
  2. The most likely cause is clear water from the a/c (normal): “Can wait”.
  3. If anything feels unsafe, slow down, pull over somewhere safe and call for help.
  4. Check for open recalls with your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Recall repairs are free at the dealer.

General help, not a diagnosis. A mechanic can tell you for sure. Costs in the app are rough estimates.
