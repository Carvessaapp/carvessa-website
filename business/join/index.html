How Carvessa works with businesses

Please read this before you create your page. It's short. The full Partner Terms are one tap away.

1 · What's free

Your page, your services and prices, photos, reviews and messages are free during our launch. Your first month in the Carvessa app is free when it launches.

2 · Payment later, only if you choose

Later we'll offer paid spots, always labeled "Sponsored". The plan is about $15–25 per ready buyer, with no monthly fee. We tell you the price in writing before you pay anything, and you can say no.

3 · Your promises

A real business, true information and real prices (you set them). Answer customers who message you. No fake or paid reviews. Treat Carvessa shoppers fairly. Never use Carvessa to agree with other businesses on prices or to split up customers.

4 · Our promises

We never sell your information. Paying never changes your reviews or our fair-price numbers. You can leave any time.

5 · When we can remove a page

For fake information, scams, abuse or breaking these rules, after a warning when we can.

We save the date, time and version of what you agreed to (draft-2026-11-20).
