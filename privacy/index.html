Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Carvessa (“we”, “us”) is an app that helps people find a car that fits their life and budget. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

2. What stays on your phone

You can use Carvessa as a guest. Your coach answers (like your budget, ZIP code and the car you drive now), your saved cars, your look (like White or Midnight) and the recalls you have seen are kept on your phone only. We do not get them.

Camera: only if you tap “Scan the VIN”, to read a car’s VIN barcode. The camera is on only while that screen is open. No picture is saved or sent to us.

Photos in car help: only if you tap the camera button in a help chat and take or choose a photo. The photo stays on your phone inside that chat so you can show it to a mechanic. It is not sent to us.

3. What we collect when you join or sign up

Account: your email and the first name you give us. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us. Your password is stored in a scrambled form by our account provider. We never see it.

Profile: the ZIP code you add in Edit profile, so prices, tax and dealers near you are right. We do not ask for a photo, address or phone number.

Your car and dealer: if you are logged in when you add your car (year, make, model and miles) or pick “My dealer”, we keep them with your account so they come back on a new phone.

Alert choices: which alerts you turned on (like recall alerts, maintenance reminders or price drops) and whether you also want them by email.

Reviews: what you write, your star rating and your tags. Reviews are public and show your first name only.

Votes and reports: when you mark a review “helpful” or report it.

Saved cars: members’ saved cars (make, model and year) are copied to our server so we can check them for recalls and bring them back on a new phone. Guests can save up to 3 cars on the phone.

Phone alerts: if you turn on recall alerts, we keep a code for your phone so we can send the alert. We do not get your phone number.

Feedback: if you send us feedback in the app, we keep the face you pick, what you write, the screen you were on and your phone type (iPhone, Android or web). If you are logged in, it is linked to your account. If you delete your account, your feedback stays but is no longer linked to you. We may use AI tools to help us read and sort feedback. We never use it to train AI or share it to sell.

Email list: if you join the list on our website (carvessa.app), we keep only your email, to tell you when the app is ready and about big news. To be taken off the list, reply to any email or write to us.

4. How we use it

To run your account, show your reviews, check your saved cars for recalls and send the alerts you asked for. To keep Carvessa safe, for example by hiding reviews that get reported. We do not use your info to decide if you can get a loan, and we never check your credit.

5. Who we share with

We never sell your info.

Service providers that run Carvessa for us: Supabase (accounts and data storage), Expo (sending phone alerts) and Google (only if you sign in with Google). They may only use your info to provide their service to us.

Public car and map data: to show weather and nearby dealers we send your ZIP code (not your name) to Zippopotam.us, OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Weather Service. To show recalls, safety ratings and gas mileage we send a car’s make, model and year (or a VIN you type or scan) to NHTSA and the EPA.

Cars for sale: to find cars near you we send your ZIP code (or the middle of the map when you tap “Search this area”) and the models you’re looking at to our server, which asks our car listings provider (Auto.dev). The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap. We don’t send your name.

Dealers: only when you ask, for example when you tap “Book a test drive” or “Contact the dealer”. We will show you what we will send before we send it.

The law: if we must by law, or to protect people from harm.

6. Ads and dealer links

Carvessa is free. To keep it free we may show ads and links to dealers and car services. They are always labeled “Ad” or “Sponsored”. We may get paid when you tap a link, visit a dealer or book a test drive.

Today we do not use ads that follow you across other apps and websites. If we ever do, we will ask you first.

Other websites have their own privacy rules. Check them before you share info there.

7. Location

We never track where you are. We only use the ZIP code you type in (or a spot you pick on the map), and only for weather, nearby dealers and cars for sale.

8. Your choices

Turn recall alerts off any time in Me. Delete any review you wrote. Delete your account any time in Me › Delete my account. This removes your account, reviews, votes, saved cars on our server and phone alerts.

You can also email us to get a copy of your info or to ask us to fix or delete it. We answer within 30 days.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your info while you have an account. When you delete your account we delete it right away. Copies in our providers’ backups are removed on their normal schedule.

10. Kids

Carvessa is not made for children under 13, and they may not make an account. If you think a child gave us info, email us and we will delete it.

11. Keeping it safe

We use secure connections and security rules so members can only change their own info. No app is 100% safe, so use a password you do not use anywhere else.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If the change is big, we will tell you in the app first.
