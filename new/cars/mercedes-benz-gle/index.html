About the Mercedes-Benz GLE

A luxury SUV with a classy inside, a smooth ride and lots of tech. Comfortable for long trips.

Smooth, quiet and steady. More comfort than sport. Feels expensive on long drives.

Tip: Options add up fast. Compare the window sticker carefully.

Why people buy it

Classic Mercedes comfortSoft, quiet and solid.
Big screensWide screens and voice control on newer ones.
Optional third rowNewer ones can seat 7 (small third row).
Safety techLots of driver assist features.

Is it right for you?

✓ you want classic luxury✓ you drive long distances✓ you can afford the upkeep

Which version to get

2020–2026 versions, simplified · check at the dealer

Buying used: which years

2020–2026Newer body and tech. Third row available. Budget for repairs after the warranty.
2016–2019Older GLE, cheaper used, but repairs can cost a lot. Get an inspection.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Is any factory warranty left?
  • Can a Mercedes shop inspect it before I buy?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
