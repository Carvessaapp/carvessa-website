About the Jeep Wrangler

The classic off-road SUV. Doors and roof come off, and it can go almost anywhere. It is loud and bumpy on the highway.

Loud and bumpy on the highway, and the steering feels loose. Off-road it’s amazing. It’s a lifestyle car more than a commuter.

Tip: A plug-in hybrid version (4xe) can drive short trips on electric power.

Buying used: which years

2018–2026Newer body, nicer inside. The 4xe plug-in had a battery recall; check it was done.
2015–2017Older body, rougher ride. Some solid-axle Jeeps can shake badly at speed (“death wobble”); test-drive on the highway.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • 2 doors or 4? Gas or 4xe plug-in?
  • Does the steering shake at highway speed?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
