About the Mitsubishi Mirage

One of the cheapest cars you can buy. It sips gas and is tiny, so parking is easy. The engine is weak and the inside is basic, so think of it as a simple way to get from A to B.

Very slow. The small 3-cylinder engine is loud when you push it, and the highway feels like work. Around town it’s simple and fine.

Tip: It has a long factory warranty when new. Ask how much warranty is left on a used one.

Why people buy it

Rock-bottom priceOne of the cheapest cars sold in America.
Sips gasAmong the best gas mileage of any gas-only car.
Tiny and easy to parkFits in spots other cars pass up.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • How much of the factory warranty is left?
  • Is it the hatchback or the G4 sedan?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
