About the Nissan Versa

A small, simple sedan that costs little to buy and to run. The ride is smooth for the size and the trunk is bigger than you expect. It is not fast, but it is easy to drive and park.

Light and easy in the city, smooth on calm roads. It feels slow when you merge onto the highway, so plan ahead.

Tip: Many 2020–2025 Versas are for sale used. A middle trim adds nicer screens and safety features for a small price.

Why people buy it

Low priceOne of the cheapest new cars, and used ones cost even less.
Easy on gasSmall engine, small gas bill. Check the exact number below (EPA).
Big trunk for its sizeGroceries, a stroller or two suitcases fit fine.
Safety tech on newer ones2020 and newer come with automatic emergency braking.

Is it right for you?

✓ it’s your first car✓ you want a low payment✓ you drive mostly in town✓ you drive for apps on a budget

Which version to get

2020–2025 trims, simplified · check at the dealer

S · The basic one. Fine for getting around, fewer extras.
SV · Adds a better screen and more comfort for a small step up in price.Best value
SR · Sportier look and bigger wheels. Nice, not needed.

Buying used: which years

2020–2025Newer, roomier body with more safety tech standard. Nissan ended the Versa after 2025.
2015–2019Older and plainer, but cheap. Most have a CVT (automatic). Ask for service records.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Has the CVT (transmission) fluid ever been changed?
  • Which trim is it: S, SV or SR?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
