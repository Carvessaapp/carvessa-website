About the Toyota Camry

A comfortable family sedan that lasts a very long time. Newer ones are hybrid only, so gas mileage is excellent.

Smooth, quiet and steady on the highway. The hybrid feels quick enough and is silent at low speeds.

Tip: Older used Camrys came with gas-only engines. Check which engine you are buying.

Buying used: which years

2025–2026All new and hybrid only. All-wheel drive is available.
2018–2024Great used pick. Most are gas 4-cylinder; a V6 and a Hybrid were also sold. Check which one.
2015–2017Older body. Cheaper, still very reliable.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Is it the Hybrid, the 4-cylinder or the V6?
  • Was it used as a rideshare or fleet car?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
