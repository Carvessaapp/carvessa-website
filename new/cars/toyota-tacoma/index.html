About the Toyota Tacoma

A tough midsize pickup known for lasting and holding its value. Great for work, off-road trips and towing small trailers.

Feels like a real truck: tall, a bit bouncy when empty. The newer turbo engines feel stronger than the old V6.

Tip: It keeps its value, so used ones cost a lot. Compare with the Ford Ranger or Chevy Colorado.

Why people buy it

Built to lastTacomas are known for running a very long time.
Holds its valueSells for a lot later, so you get more back.
Off-road readyMany trims are built for trails.
Tows and haulsGood for small trailers, boats and work.

Is it right for you?

✓ you work with tools or gear✓ you go off-road✓ you want it to last and resell well
✕ you want a cheap truck (used ones cost a lot)✕ you want a soft ride✕ gas money matters most

Buying used: which years

2024–2026All new: turbo engines, a hybrid option, big screens.
2016–2023Great used pick, mostly with a V6. Some owners say the automatic shifts a lot; test-drive on the highway.
2015Older body. Check for frame rust and service records.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Which engine and how many doors?
  • Has it been used off-road or for towing a lot?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
