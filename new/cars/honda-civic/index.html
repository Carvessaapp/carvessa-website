About the Honda Civic

A small car that feels grown-up. It is fun to drive, comfortable and well built. It holds its value well and works for almost anyone.

Smooth, quiet and sharp. It feels more expensive than it is. Fine on the highway and easy to park.

Tip: The hybrid version saves gas. Used Civics sell fast, so be ready to act.

Why people buy it

Fun to driveSteers sharp and feels solid on the road.
Lasts and holds valueCivics sell for more later than many small cars.
Grown-up insideComfortable seats and a roomy back seat.
Good on gasThe newer Hybrid is the best on gas.

Is it right for you?

✓ it’s your first car✓ you commute every day✓ you want it to last✓ you enjoy driving
✕ you need all-wheel drive✕ you need lots of cargo room✕ you want the cheapest used car (they hold value)

Which version to get

2022–2026 trims, simplified · check at the dealer

LX · Sport · Gas engine. Sport adds a sportier look.
Sport Hybrid (2025+) · More power and much better gas mileage.Best value
EX · Touring · Si · Type R · Fancier or much faster. Nice, not needed.

Buying used: which years

2022–2026Newer body, cleaner inside. Hybrid came back for 2025.
2016–2021Still a great used pick. Many have the small turbo engine; ask for oil change records.
2015Older body. Cheaper, plainer inside.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Is it the sedan or the hatchback?
  • Can I see the oil change records?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
