About the Chevy Silverado 1500

A full-size pickup that is strong, roomy and good at towing. Many trims, from basic to fancy.

Feels like a big truck: strong, a bit stiff when empty. The V8s sound great; the diesel is the best on gas.

Tip: Check the bed size and cab size. They change how much fits inside and in the bed.

Why people buy it

Strong enginesTurbo 4, V8s and a diesel to pick from.
Big bedOne of the roomiest beds in its class.
Lots for saleEasy to find used, so you can compare prices.
Parts everywhereCommon truck, so repairs are easy to find.

Is it right for you?

✓ you tow or haul for work✓ you want a V8✓ you want lots of used choices
✕ you park in tight spots✕ gas money matters most✕ you want the softest ride

Which version to get

2022–2026 trims, simplified · check at the dealer

WT · Custom · Work truck. Simple and tough.
LT · The popular mix of comfort and price.Best value
RST · LTZ · High Country · Trail Boss · ZR2 · Sporty, luxury or off-road. Nice, not needed.

Buying used: which years

2022–2026Nicer inside with a big screen. The 6.2 V8 (2021–2024) had a big engine recall; make sure it was done.
2019–2021Newer body, good value used. Ask about any engine ticking on the V8s.
2015–2018Older body, cheaper. Check service records and frame rust.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Which engine? If the 6.2 V8, was the engine recall done?
  • Which cab and bed length?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
