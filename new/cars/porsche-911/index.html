About the Porsche 911

A legendary sports car: fast, precise and fun on every drive. Still usable every day with a small back seat.

Precise, fast and exciting, yet calm when you just cruise. The back seats are tiny.

Tip: Options can add a lot to the price. Certified used ones keep a warranty.

Buying used: which years

2020–2026Newest generation. 2025 updates added a hybrid GTS.
2015–2019Older generation, still great. Get a Porsche pre-purchase inspection.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Which options does it have? (they change the price a lot)
  • Can a Porsche shop inspect it before I buy?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
