Step-by-step car help

A light came on or the car acts weird? We walk you through it, one tap at a time.

EXAMPLE
My tire light is on but the air looks fine
Is it steady, or did it flash first and then stay on?
SteadyFlashed first
Flashing first usually means a sensor problem, not a flat. Safe to drive; get the sensor checked.

Why it matters: Some lights mean “pull over now”, most don’t. Knowing which one can save you a tow, or paying for a repair you didn’t need.

Recall alerts for your car

We watch the government’s recall list for the car you own and tell you when something new comes out.

EXAMPLE
VCarvessa · now
New recall for your 2018 Honda CR-VFuel system. The fix is free at a Honda dealer. Tap to see what to do.

Why it matters: Recall letters go to the address on file, so people who bought used often never get one. The repair is free; missing it can cost you your safety.

Questions people ask

Is Carvessa really free?

Yes. Drivers never pay. We earn money from clearly labeled partners, like dealers and credit unions, only when you choose to contact them.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around. You can search cars, check prices and see what a warning light means without one, and safety steps are always open to everyone. A free account turns on the step-by-step car help chat, saves your cars and turns on recall and maintenance alerts.

Where do your prices come from?

Our own estimates from market data, plus your state’s tax and typical fees. Car facts come from the U.S. government (NHTSA and EPA). Always confirm the final price with the dealer.

Do you sell cars?

No. We help you choose and check. You buy from the dealer or seller you pick.

¿Está en español?

Sí. Toda la app está en español y en inglés. Cambia de idioma cuando quieras.

When can I get the app?

Carvessa is coming to iPhone and Android. Leave your email below and we’ll tell you the day it’s ready.
