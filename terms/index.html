1. Using Carvessa

By using Carvessa you agree to these terms and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

You must be 13 or older to make an account. Give us true info, keep your password safe, and tell us if someone else uses your account. You can delete your account any time in Me.

3. Prices, payments and estimates

Used prices, monthly payments, “What can I afford?”, cost to own and trade-in values are estimates to help you plan. They are not offers, not loan approvals and not financial advice. Real prices, rates and taxes come from the dealer and the lender. We never check your credit.

4. Car info

Recalls and safety ratings come from NHTSA. Gas mileage comes from the EPA. “About this car”, pros, cons and tips are Carvessa’s own notes. We work hard to be right, but info can be late or wrong. Check details with the dealer, and check recalls on nhtsa.gov.

5. Safety

Warning light and “car acting weird?” help is general info, not a repair or a diagnosis. If your car feels unsafe, pull over when it is safe and call for help. Always get a trained mechanic to check your car.
