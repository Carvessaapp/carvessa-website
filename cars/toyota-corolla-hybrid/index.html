About the Toyota Corolla Hybrid

Known for lasting a very long time. The hybrid gets excellent gas mileage and costs little to keep running. It is calm and easy to drive, not exciting.

Calm, quiet and easy. It’s not exciting or fast, but it never feels stressed in traffic. It starts on electric power at low speeds.

Tip: Hybrids hold their value well. A used one may cost almost as much as new, so compare both.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Is it front-wheel or all-wheel drive?
  • Has the hybrid system had any warning lights?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
