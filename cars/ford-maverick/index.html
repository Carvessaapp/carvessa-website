About the Ford Maverick

A small, affordable pickup truck. It is easy to park and the hybrid version gets car-like gas mileage. Great for weekend projects.

Drives like a car, not a truck. Smooth and easy. The turbo version is quicker and can tow more.

Tip: It is in high demand. Be ready for waitlists or markups on new ones.

Why people buy it

Truck for lessOne of the lowest-priced pickups.
Car-like gas mileageThe Hybrid is very easy on gas for a truck.
Easy to parkSmall enough for city streets and garages.
Handy bedGreat for home projects, bikes and side hustles.

Is it right for you?

✓ you do home projects✓ you have a side hustle✓ you want a truck that saves gas
✕ you tow heavy trailers✕ you need a long bed✕ you want one cheap (high demand)

Which version to get

Recent trims, simplified · check at the dealer

XL · XLT Hybrid · Best on gas. XLT adds comfort features.Best value
Lariat · Nicer inside, more tech.

Buying used: which years

2025–2026Updated. The Hybrid can now have all-wheel drive.
2022–2024First years. The Hybrid was front-wheel drive only; all-wheel drive came with the turbo. Towing depends on engine and package: check the sticker.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Is it the Hybrid or the turbo?
  • How much can this exact truck tow?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
