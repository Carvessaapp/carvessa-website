About the Kia K4

Kia’s newest small sedan, with a bold design and big screens. It replaced the Forte. It is comfortable, roomy and packed with features for the price.

Comfortable and quiet for a small car. The base engine is fine in town; the turbo GT-Line feels much quicker.

Tip: Because it’s new, used ones are rare. Compare a new K4 with a used Forte to save money.

Why people buy it

Big screensA wide screen across the dash, even on lower trims.
Roomy insideMore back-seat room than the Forte it replaced.
Packed for the priceLots of safety and comfort features standard.
Long warrantyKia’s warranty is one of the longest when new.
