About the Tesla Model Y

An electric SUV with lots of space and a quick, smooth ride. It is one of the most popular EVs, with easy fast charging on road trips.

Quick and smooth, with a firm ride. Quiet on the highway. Almost everything is on the big screen.

Tip: Insurance can cost more than for a gas SUV. Get a quote before you buy.

Why people buy it

Lots of spaceBig trunk plus a front trunk. Roomy back seat.
No gasCharging at home costs much less than gas for most people.
Easy road tripsTesla Superchargers are everywhere on highways.
Little upkeepNo oil changes. Mostly tires and brakes.

Is it right for you?

✓ you have a family and can charge at home✓ you drive for apps a lot✓ you want low running costs
✕ you can’t charge at home or work✕ you want a soft ride✕ you want cheap insurance (it can cost more)

Buying used: which years

2025–2026Updated: quieter, softer ride, new look.
2020–2024Cheaper used. A small third row was offered on some. Ask for battery health and real range.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • What’s the battery health and the real range today?
  • Does it come with a charging cable?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
