About the Buick Encore GX

A small SUV with a quieter, more upscale feel than most at its price. It is comfortable and offers all-wheel drive.

Soft and quiet. The small turbo engine is fine for daily driving, not quick. It feels calm on rough roads.

Tip: It shares parts with the Chevy Trailblazer, so repairs are easy to find.
