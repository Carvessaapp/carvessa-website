About the Land Rover Range Rover

A top-level luxury SUV that is comfortable on the road and strong off it. Very fancy and very expensive.

Floats like a cloud and is very quiet. Feels like a private jet on wheels.

Tip: Repair costs can be very high. Buy certified or with an extended warranty.

Why people buy it

Top-level luxuryOne of the most luxurious SUVs made.
Goes off-road tooVery capable, even if most never leave the pavement.
Iconic lookInstantly recognized.
Smooth powerStrong 6-cylinder, V8 or plug-in hybrid.

Is it right for you?

✓ you want the best luxury✓ you can afford big repair bills✓ you buy new with a warranty
✕ you want low repair costs✕ you want top reliability ratings✕ you buy used without a warranty

Which version to get

2022–2026 versions, simplified · check at the dealer

SE · Already very luxurious.Best value
Autobiography · SV · Even more luxury. Very expensive.
Plug-in hybrid (P550e) · Short trips on electric power.

Buying used: which years

2022–2026All new and very modern. Buy with a warranty if you can.
2015–2021Cheaper used, but many owners report costly repairs (air suspension, electronics). Get an inspection and consider a warranty.

Questions to ask the dealer

  • Is any factory or extended warranty left?
  • Has the air suspension had any repairs?
  • Which trim is it, exactly?
  • Any open recalls, and were they fixed?
  • Can I see the service records?
